# Abschluss 1.3.2

Windows installation and uninstall now resolve paths consistently when APPDATA or LOCALAPPDATA is absent or empty. Explicit environment paths must be absolute. Windows known-folder resolution replaces the former Racket preference-directory fallback.

Uninstall resolves and validates shortcut paths before removing unchanged recorded payloads. An invalid path fails during preflight, retaining payloads and the installation receipt. Shortcut target, recorded hash, Programs-directory containment and reparse-point checks remain in force. Modified and additional files remain preserved.

The exact matrix engine, v6 primary pmp judge, independent Racket cross-check and Book v7 source are retained. Exact rational and integer arithmetic, record-count rejection before allocation, and recorded Book route handling keep their reviewed source bytes.

The earlier 1.3.1 release, source variants and failed owned tests are retained. Reader, count-model and RECORDCOUNT source-controller proof archives retain their stated hypotheses and boundaries. Native/compiler, OS and complete-judge executable correspondence remain separate formal obligations.
